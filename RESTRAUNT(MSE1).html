Our Philosophy

An Ode to Sensory Harmony

Established in 2018, Aura Bistro & Bar emerged from a vision to redefine modern bistro culture. We unify timeless European techniques with bold botanical infusions, presenting guests with sensory harmony from the first pour to the final bite.

Our produce is hand-picked daily from ethical heirloom farms, our seafood is wild-caught from certified sustainable fishermen, and all pastas and bread doughs ferment naturally for 48 hours.
